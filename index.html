Policy reference & your obligations AML/CFT

By signing, you confirm the following personal obligations. These are aligned with BIT's AML/CFT policy and applicable Singapore requirements — MAS Notice PSN02 (Digital Payment Token Service), the CDSA and TSOFA. The full policy governs.

01

Read & understand the policy

I have read and understood BIT's AML/CFT Policy and understand how it applies to my role and responsibilities.

02

Complete training

I will complete the AML/CFT training assigned to me, covering (a) AML/CFT laws and regulations and, in particular, CDD measures and the detecting and reporting of suspicious transactions; (b) prevailing money-laundering and terrorist-financing techniques and trends; and (c) BIT's internal AML/CFT policies and my roles and responsibilities. (MAS Notice PSN02, para 17.7)

03

Escalate suspicion

Where I know or have reasonable grounds to suspect money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, or other financial crime, I will promptly escalate it internally to the compliance officer / MLRO through the designated channel — at the point of suspicion, without waiting for certainty — so that a suspicious transaction report (STR) can be assessed and, where required, filed with the Suspicious Transaction Reporting Office (STRO). (CDSA; TSOFA)

04

No tipping-off

I understand that "tipping-off" — disclosing to a customer or any other person that an STR has been or may be filed, or that an investigation is underway — is a criminal offence under section 57 of the CDSA, and I will not do so. I will treat such matters strictly on a need-to-know basis. (CDSA, s.57)

05

Cooperate & keep records

I will cooperate fully with the compliance function and any internal or regulatory investigation, follow applicable record-keeping requirements, and avoid any conduct that could assist money laundering or breach sanctions.

06

Annual renewal

I understand this attestation must be renewed annually, or earlier upon any material change to the policy, and that non-compliance may result in disciplinary action and potential legal liability.

Official sources: BIT AML/CFT Policy (bit.com/aml); MAS Notice PSN02 & Guidelines (mas.gov.sg); Corruption, Drug Trafficking and Other Serious Crimes Act (CDSA); Terrorism (Suppression of Financing) Act (TSOFA).

01

阅读并理解政策

本人已阅读并理解 BIT 的《AML/CFT 政策》，并理解其如何适用于本人的岗位与职责。

02

完成培训

本人将完成分配给本人的 AML/CFT 培训，内容涵盖：(a) AML/CFT 法律法规，特别是客户尽职调查（CDD）措施，以及可疑交易的识别与报告；(b) 洗钱与恐怖主义融资的当前手法与趋势；(c) BIT 的内部 AML/CFT 政策及本人的职责。（MAS Notice PSN02 第 17.7 段）

03

上报可疑情形

当本人知悉或有合理理由怀疑存在洗钱、恐怖主义融资、规避制裁或其他金融犯罪时，本人将在产生怀疑之时（无需等待确证）通过指定渠道及时向合规官／MLRO 进行内部上报，以便评估可疑交易报告（STR）并在需要时向可疑交易报告办公室（STRO）提交。（CDSA；TSOFA）

04

不得通风报信

本人理解"通风报信"（tipping-off）——即向客户或任何其他人披露已经或可能提交 STR、或正在进行调查——依据 CDSA 第 57 条构成刑事罪，本人不会实施此类行为，并将严格按"必要知悉"原则处理相关事宜。（CDSA 第 57 条）

05

配合调查与记录留存

本人将全力配合合规部门及任何内部或监管调查，遵守适用的记录留存要求，并避免任何可能协助洗钱或违反制裁的行为。

06

年度更新

本人理解本声明须每年更新一次，或在政策发生重大变更时提前更新；违规可能导致纪律处分及潜在法律责任。
